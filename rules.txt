Gameplay Overview

    Initial Deal:

        Each player gets 2 cards face up.

        The dealer gets 1 card face up and 1 card face down (the “hole” card).

    Player's Turn:

        You may choose to:

            Hit – Take another card.

            Stand – Keep your current hand.

            Double Down – Double your bet, take one more card, then stand.

            Split – If you have two cards of the same value, split them into two hands (requires an additional bet).

            Surrender (if allowed) – Give up your hand and get half your bet back.

    Dealer's Turn:

        The dealer reveals the hole card.

        Must hit until reaching at least 17.

            In most casinos, dealer must hit on soft 17 (Ace + 6 = 17), but rules vary.

        If the dealer busts (goes over 21), all remaining players win.

Winning

    Your hand is closer to 21 than the dealer’s without busting → You win.

    Your hand equals 21 with first 2 cards (Ace + 10-card) → Blackjack (usually pays 3:2).

    Your hand ties with the dealer → Push (you get your bet back).

    Your hand goes over 21 → Bust → You lose.

    Dealer has a better hand → You lose.
